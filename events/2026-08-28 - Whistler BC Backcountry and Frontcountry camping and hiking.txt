🏕️ What This Is All About
A 9-night BC adventure splitting time between the alpine and the valley — Whistler area, glaciers, granite, and big objectives 🏔️✨

🥾 Phase 1 — Wedgemount Lake (backcountry) | 5 nights
We pack in heavy, then base-camp at one of BC's most jaw-dropping alpine lakes. Days are for radiating out — ridge scrambles, summit pushes, and lake lounging. See the Hike/Scramble ideas below 👇

🌲 Phase 2 — Nairn Falls (frontcountry) | 4 nights
Back to the car, back to cold drinks and camp chairs 🍻. Day trips around the area — I haven't scouted the objectives yet, so this is wide open. Drop your ideas, I'm all ears 👂

🚗 Roll back to Calgary on September 7th, tanned, tired, and stoked.
