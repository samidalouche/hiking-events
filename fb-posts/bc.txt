## 2026

```
Hello. I will be visiting the Whistler area from Calgary, AB.

August 29 (Sat) -> Sep 1st (Tue) -- 4 nights: Nairn Falls Campground (frontcountry camping).
Sep 2nd (Wed) to Sep 7th (Mon) -- 5 nights: Wedgemount Lake (backcountry camping).

I posted a couple years ago on this group during my last visit and some people suggested gems such as Slalok Mountain and Tszil Mountain in the Joffre Lakes area.

So I am doing another post in case you have scrambling gems to suggest in the Nairn Falls and Wedgemount Lake areas. Feel free to suggest regular hiking ideas too, I am after great views.

I would love to share the trail with some of you too, if you want to join! I am into early-to-super-early starts (read: I find every excuse to plan an adventure that starts before sunrise) and love taking my time for pictures and fun. I zoom during the boring sections.

To get an idea of what I am after, here are a couple hikes/scrambles I did during my previous visit in the Squamish area, that I loved
- The Black Tusk & Panorama Ridge
- Joffre Lakes, Slalok Mountain, and Tszil Mountain

For Wedgemount Lake area, the ideas that I have gathered so far:
- Armchair Traverse (Mount Cook -> Mount Weart)
- Pakhurst Mountain
- Rethel Mountain
- Wedge Mountain

PS: pictures for attention, all taken from my various adventures in the Rockies this summer.
```
