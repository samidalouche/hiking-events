# Multi-Day Trips — Interest / Heads-Up Post

A light, friendly post to drop in various FB groups **once in a while** to gauge interest in joining next summer's multi-day camping / hut trips.

**How to use it:**
- Attach a few pictures from recent hikes/trips (the post is built to sit under a photo dump 👆).
- It's intentionally evergreen: it talks about "next summer" and syncing in "November/December" for the "January launch", so it stays valid year to year — no dates to update.

## FB Post

### English

```
📸 Okay, it's been a hot minute since I posted here… so consider this a little photo dump to make up for it 👆

Whether you're all about frontcountry camping, big hiking/scrambling days, or the whole combo, let's connect 🫶 I'm always happy to meet more humans who love waking up in the mountains — even if some of us take "waking up" a little too literally with 3am alpine starts 😅

Quick reality check: this summer's camping is already fully booked — we sorted it all out back in January 🎟️ — so this is NOT a "come this weekend" post. It's a heads-up for *next* summer 👀

Here's the deal: most national park sites open for reservation in January, and it's a bit of a Hunger Games situation 🏹. So if there's even a chance you'd want to join a multi-day camping trip next year, let's sync up in November or December — that way we can grab sites together the second the booking window opens 🚀

What are these trips actually like? Think chill, flexible, go-with-the-flow 😎. Do your own thing by day — chase a peak, bring a bike, take a mellow recovery day, nap in a meadow, float in a lake, or just soak up the campsite — then reconvene in the evening for food, laughs, and campfire hangs 🔥🍲. And to be clear: coming *just* for the camping is 100% a thing — lazy lakeside mornings, a camp chair, a good book, and time around the fire is every bit as legit as bagging a summit ⛺️☕️. Honestly, hardly anyone hikes every single day (except me, obviously 🙃), and that's the beauty of it: with a mix of motivations in the group, there's always someone up for whatever you're feeling that day — so nobody's stuck going solo 🧡
👉 Full rundown here: https://www.alberta-hiking-resources.org/hike-organizers/sami/multi-day-trip

Full disclosure though 🫠: I will find literally any excuse to plan a 12–15 hour sunrise mission starting at 3am ⛰️. I would LOVE to find more people who share my particular brand of craziness 🤪 — but if that sounds like your personal nightmare, you'll be in excellent company: part of my crew regularly stages a mutiny and takes a well-earned rest day — or just posts up at camp all day — far from my sufferfests 😴🏖️

So… tempted? Drop a comment or send me a message 💬

📷 Want the ongoing highlight reel? Follow the adventures on Instagram: @samidalouche → https://www.instagram.com/samidalouche/

Can't wait to share some trails (and campfires) with you! 🥾💛
```
